# sahiraahi.co.in

Pre-launch website for Sahiraahi, a women-first travel companion for India. Static HTML, hosted on GitHub Pages, custom domain `sahiraahi.co.in` (see `CNAME`).

- `index.html` is the whole site (inline CSS, Fraunces + Inter from Google Fonts)
- `404.html` is the not-found page
- `assets/` holds the logo, icons and the social share image (`og.png`, 1200×630)

The site collects no data: no forms, no analytics, no cookies. Contact is Instagram only ([@sahiraahi](https://www.instagram.com/sahiraahi/)).

## Copy rules

Only claim what the product supports at launch: every listing shows who checked it and when; WiFi measured at cafés and eateries; clinics confirmed by phone; "women say" from women who visited. No diet or allergen claims, and never call a place "safe". Launch is Pune, Vijayawada and Guntur at the end of November 2026.

Add the privacy policy and terms pages here once they're out of legal review, and before any form, sign-up or analytics goes live.
